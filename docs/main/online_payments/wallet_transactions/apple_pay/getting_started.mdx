# Apple Pay™ Getting Started

Apple Pay™ enables fast, secure payments for customers using Apple devices. With Pay Theory's integration, you can accept Apple Pay™ payments through our simple gateway integration.

---

## Overview

Apple Pay™ allows customers to pay using cards saved to their Apple Wallet. When integrated with Pay Theory:

- Works on Safari browser and all Apple devices
- Customers authenticate with Touch ID, Face ID, or device passcode
- Payment information is tokenized for enhanced security
- Transactions process through your existing Pay Theory merchant account

---

## Requirements

### General Requirements

To accept Apple Pay™ with Pay Theory, you'll need:

- An active Pay Theory merchant account

### Web Requirements

For Apple Pay™ on your website:

- SSL certificate (HTTPS required)
- Domain verification through Pay Theory portal

### Native iOS Requirements

For Apple Pay™ in iOS apps:

- Apple Pay™ Merchant ID configured
- Payment processing certificate from Pay Theory
- iOS app registered in Pay Theory portal
