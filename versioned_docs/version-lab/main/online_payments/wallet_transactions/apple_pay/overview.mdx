# Apple Pay Overview

Enable Apple Pay™ to provide customers with a seamless, secure payment experience on Apple devices.

***

## What is Apple Pay?

Apple Pay allows customers to pay with a single touch using cards stored in their Apple Wallet. When integrated with Pay Theory:

- **Eliminates checkout friction** with Touch ID or Face ID authentication
- **Increases security** through device-specific tokenization
- **Boosts conversion rates** with faster, simpler checkout
- **Works across devices** including iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch

***

## How It Works

1. **Customer clicks** the Apple Pay button
2. **Device authenticates** via Touch ID, Face ID, or passcode
3. **Apple generates** a secure payment token
4. **Your server validates** with Pay Theory
5. **Pay Theory processes** the payment

***

## Browser & Device Support

Apple Pay is available on:
- **Safari** on Mac (with Touch ID or authenticated Apple Watch)
- **Safari** on iPhone and iPad
- **In-app** on iOS apps
- **NOT supported** on Chrome, Firefox, or Edge

:::note Browser Limitations
Apple Pay only works in Safari. Consider offering alternative payment methods for users on other browsers.
:::
