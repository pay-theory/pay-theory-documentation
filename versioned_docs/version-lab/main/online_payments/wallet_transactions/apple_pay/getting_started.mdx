# Apple Pay™ Getting Started

Apple Pay™ provides a seamless payment experience for customers using Apple devices. With Pay Theory's integration, you can accept Apple Pay™ payments through a simple, secure API.

---

## Overview

Apple Pay™ allows customers to make payments using cards stored in their Apple Wallet. When integrated with Pay Theory:

- Customers authenticate with Touch ID, Face ID, or device passcode
- Payment information is tokenized for enhanced security
- Transactions process through your existing Pay Theory merchant account

---

## Requirements

To accept Apple Pay™ with Pay Theory, you'll need:

- An active Pay Theory merchant account

**Web:**

- SSL certificate on your website (HTTPS required)
- Domain verification with Apple

**Native iOS:**

- Apple Developer account
- CSR file from Apple Developer Portal
