# Google Pay™ Getting Started

Google Pay™ enables fast, secure payments for customers across multiple browsers and devices. With Pay Theory's integration, you can accept Google Pay™ payments through our simple gateway integration.

***

## Overview

Google Pay™ allows customers to pay using cards saved to their Google account. When integrated with Pay Theory:

- Works across Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge browsers
- Supports both mobile and desktop experiences
- Payment information is tokenized for enhanced security
- Transactions process through your existing Pay Theory merchant account

***

## Requirements

To accept Google Pay™ with Pay Theory, you'll need:

- An active Pay Theory merchant account
- Google account for business use
- SSL certificate on your website (HTTPS required)
- Registration in Google Pay & Wallet Console

***

## Getting Started with Google

Before implementing Google Pay™, you'll need to register with Google:

1. **Create a business profile** - Register your business information
2. **Configure Pay Theory as your gateway** - Specify Pay Theory in your integration settings
3. **Add your website** - Google will verify your SSL certificate
4. **Accept Terms of Service** - Complete the registration process

For detailed setup instructions, refer to Google's official documentation:

<a href="https://developers.google.com/pay/api/web/guides/setup" class="button button--secondary button--md" target="_blank">
  Google Pay Developer Guide →
</a>

***

## Testing

Google Pay™ provides a TEST environment for development:
- No registration required for testing
- Use test card numbers provided by Google
- Switch to PRODUCTION when ready to accept live payments
