# Google Pay Overview

Integrate Google Pay™ to provide customers with a fast, secure checkout experience using cards saved to their Google Account.

***

## What is Google Pay?

Google Pay enables one-click payments by tokenizing payment cards stored in a customer's Google Account. When integrated with Pay Theory:

- **Increases conversion** by reducing checkout friction
- **Enhances security** through payment tokenization
- **Supports all major card networks** including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover
- **Works seamlessly** across mobile and desktop web experiences

***

## How It Works

1. **Customer clicks** the Google Pay button
2. **Google displays** saved payment methods
3. **User selects** a card and confirms
4. **Google generates** a secure payment token
5. **Pay Theory processes** the tokenized payment

***

## Browser & Device Compatibility

Google Pay is supported on:
- **Chrome** (version 61+)
- **Safari** (version 11.1+)
- **Firefox** (version 60+)
- **Edge** (version 79+)
- **Android devices** with Google Play Services
- **iOS devices** with Google Pay app

:::tip Wide Compatibility
Unlike Apple Pay, Google Pay works across multiple browsers and platforms, making it accessible to more customers.
:::
